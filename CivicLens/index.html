How CivicLens AI Works

1. Report an Issue

Citizens submit complaints with a description, location and optional photo.

2. Smart Analysis

The system analyses the complaint, suggests a priority, generates a summary and checks for similar reports.

3. Department Assignment

The complaint is directed to the suggested responsible department for review.

4. Authority Action

Authorities review complaints, update progress and provide resolution details.

5. Citizen Feedback

Citizens track the complaint and provide feedback after resolution.
